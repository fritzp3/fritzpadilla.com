## The brief

Nusura, Inc., a Denver-based emergency management consulting firm, needed a corporate website and blog that showed what the company does best, including its video and creative capabilities. The team also needed to keep both sites up to date themselves, adding news, posts and media without relying on a developer for every change.

## The approach

Both the corporate website and the blog were built on Drupal, the open source content management system.

- **A content system the team could run.** Drupal gave Nusura's staff an editor for pages, blog posts and media, so they could publish on their own schedule.
- **Room for video and creative work.** The layouts were designed to showcase video and visual work alongside the firm's services, so visitors could see Nusura's capabilities as well as read about them.
- **One platform for both sites.** Building the website and the blog on the same system kept them consistent and made them easier to maintain.

## The outcome

Nusura launched a corporate website and blog that present its core competencies clearly and give its video and creative work a prominent place. With Drupal behind both sites, the team could keep its content current on its own.
