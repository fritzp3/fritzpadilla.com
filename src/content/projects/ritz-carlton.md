## The brief

Ritz-Carlton Destination Club needed an interactive digital sign for its guests and members. Working under contract with Four Winds Interactive, a digital signage company, the task was to create a touch-screen application that people could walk up to and use with no instructions.

The experience had to match the polish of the Ritz-Carlton brand and feel familiar from the first touch.

## The approach

The application was designed to work like an iPhone app, the touch interface most visitors already knew.

- **Familiar touch navigation.** Large touch targets, simple screens and tap-to-open sections let guests move through the content the same way they would on their phone.
- **Designed for a public screen.** Content was organized into short, clear sections, so a passing guest could find what they wanted in a few taps and step away.
- **Brand-first presentation.** The look and motion followed the Ritz-Carlton style, so the sign felt like part of the property rather than a piece of technology.

## The outcome

Ritz-Carlton Destination Club received a touch-screen digital sign that guests could use right away, with navigation as intuitive as a smartphone app. The project brought an interactive, on-brand experience to the club's physical spaces.
