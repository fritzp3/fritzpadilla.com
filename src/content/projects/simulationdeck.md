## The brief

Nusura, a Denver-based team of emergency management training experts, needed a flagship digital tool for its exercises. In a real disaster, responders and public information officers face a flood of public reaction across news and social media. Training for that pressure is hard without a realistic, controlled environment.

The goal was SimulationDeck: an enterprise web application that could simulate the public's response to real-world events and disasters, so agencies could practice communicating and responding as a scenario unfolds.

## The approach

The platform was built as a secure, closed environment that mirrors the channels people turn to during an emergency.

- **Realistic simulated channels.** The application recreates the kinds of news, social media and public feedback that responders would see, so exercises feel close to a live event.
- **Controlled by the exercise team.** Exercise staff can feed in simulated public reaction as the scenario develops, raising the pressure and testing how participants respond.
- **Built for enterprise use.** The platform supports multiple organizations and exercises, with access limited to the people taking part, so sensitive training content stays private.

## The outcome

SimulationDeck became Nusura's flagship simulation tool, giving emergency management agencies a realistic place to rehearse public communication before a real disaster strikes. It turned a difficult-to-practice part of emergency response into something teams can train for again and again.
